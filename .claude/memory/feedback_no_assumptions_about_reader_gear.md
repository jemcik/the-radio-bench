ch4.3, `rfP9`: «…впливає на рівень опромінення більше, ніж **куплений** підсилювач»
("more than the amplifier **you bought**"). User: how do you know it's bought? I
may have built it myself or been given it — «писати базуючись на таких
припущеннях — це ідіотизм». Fixed → «ніж **сам** підсилювач» / "than the
amplifier itself". Also fixed `lightP5` EN «no earth resistance you can **buy**»
→ "low enough" (you might build your own ground).

**Why:** hams routinely home-build, kit-build, buy used, or inherit gear.
Assuming a purchase (or a price, or "your expensive X") is both wrong for many
readers and needless — the sentence's point never depends on it.

**How to apply:**
- Never write «the amplifier you bought / your expensive antenna analyser /
  the rig you paid for / what you can buy». Say «the amplifier itself / your
  amplifier / an amplifier» — the acquisition is irrelevant to the point.
- **«own» is FINE** — you own gear however you got it (built, gifted, bought).
  The banned thing is the *acquisition method or cost*, not possession.
- **«buy» is fine for genuine consumables** — fuses, solder, wire: you can't
  build a fuse, so «the fuses you buy in Ukraine» is not an assumption.

Same family as [[feedback_no_condescension_explain_standards]] and
[[feedback_glossary_no_reader_position]]: the umbrella rule is **don't build a
sentence on an assumption about the reader's situation** (their competence, their
chapter, their wallet). When flagged once, grep the whole chapter for the class.
